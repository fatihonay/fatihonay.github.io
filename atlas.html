How to read this atlas

The brain is the MNI ICBM152 template: an average of many adult MRI scans, used as a common reference space in neuroimaging. Coloured regions come from the CerebrA atlas, which divides each hemisphere into 51 anatomical regions (cortical areas, deep structures and cerebellum). Colours group regions by lobe.

The electrodes follow the international 10-20 system, the standard way of placing EEG electrodes on the scalp. In the 3D view each electrode is shown where it projects onto the cortex. For each electrode, the panel lists the regions within 1 cm of that point. Keep in mind that EEG measures a blurred sum of activity from a much larger area, so this is "what lies beneath", not "the only source of the signal".
