Bio

I am Dr. Research Assistant in the Mechatronics Engineering Department at Bursa Technical University, Turkey. I received a Ph.D. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Izmir Institute of Technology in 2025, with a thesis on a computational brain connectivity framework for evaluating alterations in brain function using EEG. My research applies signal processing, statistical modeling, pattern recognition and machine learning to complex dynamic systems, with a focus on multi-channel EEG analysis and neural biomarkers for clinical and cognitive neuroscience. I have been a visiting researcher at GIPSA-Lab, Université Grenoble Alpes (2024) and CIMeC, University of Trento (2026).
